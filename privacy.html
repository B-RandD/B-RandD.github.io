Privacy policy

Executive Function backups. Last updated 13 November 2026.

In short

Nothing is collected or shared. The tool runs on its owner's own computer, has no other users, and talks to no one but Google, to keep the owner's backups in the owner's own Google Drive.

What it accesses in Google Drive

Only what the drive.file permission allows: the folder "Executive Function backups" and the files the tool itself put there. It never sees any other file in the Drive. It uploads sealed (encrypted) backups and the program that opens them, reads back their size and checksum to check each upload, deletes an upload that arrived damaged (to send it again), and moves a replaced copy of that program to the Drive's trash.

What it keeps, and where

The Google sign-in that lets the backup run each night (a refresh token) is kept in a file on the owner's computer and nowhere else. The backups it uploads are encrypted before they leave that computer; the key that opens them is not on the computer, and is never sent to Google or anyone else.

What it shares

Nothing. No data is sold, shared, or sent to any server other than Google's. There are no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The tool's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can end the tool's access at any time from their Google Account, under Third-party apps & services, and delete its folder from their Drive.
